Meet your coach

Big-stage experience.
Personal coaching.

Peter Hill has been teaching swimming since 1993 and currently coaches Larne Swimming Club. He has coached athletes at three Olympic Games — Beijing, Tokyo and Paris — and six Commonwealth Games, as well as a European 50m backstroke champion.

That experience spans the entire swimming journey, from helping a child become comfortable in the water to preparing athletes for the world's biggest competitions. At Peter Hill Swimming, that same attention to detail is brought to every lesson.

Questions?

Before your first lesson.

What age can my child start?

Lessons are available for children aged 3 and up.

Does my child need to be able to swim already?

No. The programme is designed to support children from complete non-swimmer through to confident and club-ready swimmers.

How much do lessons cost?

Lessons are £10 per lesson. A monthly payment option offers a 10% saving.

Where are the lessons held?

At Belfast Royal Academy pool, 5–17 Cliftonville Road, Belfast, BT14 6JL.
