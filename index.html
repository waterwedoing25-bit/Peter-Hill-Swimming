Meet Coach Peter

Big-time experience, small-group care

Peter Hill has been teaching swimming since 1993 and currently coaches Larne Swimming Club. He has coached athletes at three Olympic Games — Beijing, Tokyo and Paris — and six Commonwealth Games, including Glasgow, Birmingham, Gold Coast, Delhi and Melbourne, plus a European 50m backstroke champion.

Peter is hands-on at every level of the sport, from a child's very first time in the water to athletes racing on the world stage — and he loves nothing more than helping kids fall in love with swimming.

  • Olympic Games: Beijing, Tokyo, Paris
  • Commonwealth Games: Glasgow, Birmingham, Gold Coast, Delhi, Melbourne
  • European Champion coached — 50m Backstroke
  • Teaching since 1993 · Larne Swimming Club coach
